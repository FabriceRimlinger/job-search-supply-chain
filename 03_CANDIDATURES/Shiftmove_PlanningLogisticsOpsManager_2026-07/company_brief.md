# company_brief - Shiftmove

## Secteur
B2B SaaS - fleet management and mobility operations software. AI-powered platform connecting real-time vehicle data with software for procurement, cost control, compliance, and cleaner-mobility transition. Categorically adjacent to telematics/IoT and field service management.

## Taille & structure
- Profitable, PE-backed (Battery Ventures) scale-up - fastest-growing fleet management platform in Europe.
- 730,000+ vehicles managed, 1M+ drivers, 25,000+ fleet customers, 60,000 prosumer customers.
- Born from Avrios + Vimcar merger (July 2023). Recent French acquisitions: Optimum Automotive + Ocean (geolocation) - doubling down on France as one of Europe's largest fleet markets.
- Multi-brand strategy: Vimcar (DACH logbook/GPS), Optimum (FR fleet, 100+ vehicles), Ocean (FR GPS tracking), Avrios (pan-EU fleet mgmt).
- Leadership: Francine Gervazio (CEO), Wouter Hendriks (CFO), Felix Schmidt (CPTO), Christian Siewek (CCO), Manuel Schuler (GM France), Olga Long (VP Operations), Jason Kolt (VP Marketing).
- 30+/40 nationalities, English as working language.
- Ambition: 1M vehicles by 2027.

## Valeurs & culture
- "Make It Happen" - ownership, autonomy, real responsibility, freedom to design and improve.
- "Better Together: Fleet Technology That Thinks. People Who Care." - collaborative, customer-care oriented.
- "You're in the driver's seat. We're the engine."
- Systems thinkers, data-driven, commercially aware, influence without relying on direct authority.
- Fast-paced, evolving environment (PE-backed scale-up pace).
- "Apply anyway if unsure" - openness to non-canonical profiles, cross-domain backgrounds welcomed.

## Enjeux supply chain / operations spécifiques
- **Post-acquisition integration** Ocean + Optimum into one French operating model - the core mandate of this role.
- **Praxedo business ownership** - establish as standard field operations platform across France (FSM: scheduling, dispatch, mobile field app, ERP/CRM connectors, AI route optimization). Praxedo = 20 years, 1,500 clients, 65,000 daily users, 150,000 interventions/day, Gartner MQ 2022.
- **Hardware lifecycle** governance: procurement, inventory control, reverse logistics, asset management for field hardware (devices, GPS units).
- **Cross-functional handoffs** Planning ↔ Logistics ↔ Customer Support ↔ Customer Success to cut friction and resolution times.
- **KPI framework + executive reporting** driving continuous improvement culture (Lean).
- 70% of European businesses still manage fleets manually with spreadsheets - the market gap Shiftmove addresses.

- Fleet Intelligence Report 2026 published - AI for fleet managers as forward growth lever.
