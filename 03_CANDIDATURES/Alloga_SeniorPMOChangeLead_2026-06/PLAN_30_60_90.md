# 30/60/90-Day Plan — Alloga Senior PMO / Change Lead Mission

**Main stakeholder**: Jeremy Ehlers (CIO / Senior Director)
**Junior leader to coach**: Baptiste (title to be confirmed)
**Scope**: Transformation management / crisis management — Marseille team
**Authority status**: Consultant — no line management decision power (for now)
**Dual mandate**: Above the line (team image and perception) + Below the line (ways of working reengineering)

---

## Guiding Principles

- **Listen before prescribing**: 9 months of underperformance = distrust of "fixers". Arrive without a pre-packaged solution.
- **Baptiste first**: the team follows its leader. If Baptiste resists or is bypassed, the mission fails. His buy-in is the key.
- **Visible quick wins**: every week, one small but visible improvement — builds credibility and breaks the victim dynamic.
- **Co-creation**: artefacts built with the team get adopted. Those imposed from outside get ignored.
- **No grand launch speech**: behaviour, not declaration.

---

## DAYS 1-30 — Listen, map, earn trust

### Objective
Understand the real situation (not Jeremy's version of it). Identify root causes. Earn Baptiste's and the team's trust before prescribing anything.

### Actions

**Week 1 — Pure observation**
- [ ] 1:1 with Baptiste (Day 1): active listening — his read of the situation, his frustrations, his priorities. No solutions offered.
- [ ] Observe 2-3 project meetings without intervening — note patterns (who speaks, who stays silent, where tensions surface, how decisions are made)
- [ ] Request access to existing governance materials (docs, RACI, current tools) — silent audit
- [ ] Map active projects: list, perceived status, responsible PM, client, deadline

**Week 2 — Root cause mapping**
- [ ] Individual 30-min interviews with each PM — open questions: "What's blocking you?", "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
- [ ] Identify the 2-3 highest-risk projects (client or milestone at risk) — quick win candidates
- [ ] Map external interfaces: other leaders, clients, cross-functional teams — identify who perceives the team negatively and why

**Weeks 3-4 — Synthesis and shared plan**
- [ ] Share diagnostic with Baptiste first (before Jeremy): show him what we observed together, let him validate or correct
- [ ] Co-build with Baptiste the 3 priorities for month 2 — he is the author, not Fabrice
- [ ] Present to Jeremy: factual diagnostic + 3 action tracks, timeline, success indicators
- [ ] Agree weekly check-in format with Jeremy (30 min, structured agenda)

### Day 30 Deliverables
- [ ] Active project map (structured list + real status)
- [ ] Shared diagnostic (1-page doc) — validated by Baptiste, presented to Jeremy
- [ ] Month 2 action plan co-built with Baptiste
- [ ] 1 visible quick win (e.g. structured kick-off meeting on the highest-risk project)

---

## DAYS 31-60 — Embed, build artefacts, coach Baptiste

### Objective
Install the foundational practices. Build artefacts with the team. Coach Baptiste in real time on his meetings and deliverables. First results visible to clients and other leaders.

### Actions

**PMO artefacts — co-built with the team**
- [ ] Physical kick-off meeting template (agenda, mandatory attendees, expected outputs)
- [ ] Milestone tracking template (lightweight format, not Outlook — to be defined with the team based on available tools)
- [ ] Client "playback" template with formal sign-off gate (solution restitution document + written client validation)
- [ ] Clarified RACI by project type — who does what, who approves, who is informed

**Baptiste coaching — weekly**
- [ ] 1h/week session: debrief on his meetings, decisions and communications
- [ ] Observe Baptiste in meetings, give feedback after the session (never in public)
- [ ] Work on his posture: move out of victim mode, communicate "above the line" with other leaders
- [ ] Co-write his key communications to Jeremy and senior stakeholders

**Above the line — team image**
- [ ] Identify 2-3 external leaders with degraded perception of the team — proactive outreach (Fabrice + Baptiste together)
- [ ] Create a "win communication" format: every closed project or reached milestone → visible message to stakeholders
- [ ] Propose to Jeremy a monthly team update where Baptiste presents progress (gives him positive visibility)

**Governance — application**
- [ ] Accompany PMs physically in their first kick-offs using the new template
- [ ] Check template usage 1 week after introduction — individual coaching if not used
- [ ] Install milestone tracking as a weekly routine (15 min per PM)

### Day 60 Deliverables
- [ ] PMO artefact pack v1 (kick-off, milestone tracking, playback, RACI) — used on at least 3 projects
- [ ] Baptiste: 1 above-the-line communication written and sent to a senior leader
- [ ] Mid-point report to Jeremy: stabilised projects vs still-at-risk, Baptiste coaching trends
- [ ] 1 project closed with formal client sign-off

---

## DAYS 61-90 — Embed, measure, prepare for the future Director

### Objective
Practices hold without Fabrice's presence. Baptiste gains autonomy. The team is perceived differently internally. The ground is ready for the future Head/Director of Projects.

### Actions

**Autonomy test**
- [ ] Progressively reduce presence in project meetings — let PMs and Baptiste apply practices independently, observe from outside
- [ ] Identify PMs who have internalised the new practices vs those who revert — targeted coaching on relapses
- [ ] Baptiste runs 1 governance meeting solo with Fabrice as silent observer

**Measure impact**
- [ ] PMO artefact usage rate (kick-off, milestone tracking, playback) — target: 100% of active projects
- [ ] Number of projects with recorded formal client sign-off
- [ ] External perception: gather informal feedback from other leaders (vs Day 1)
- [ ] Status of at-risk projects identified at Day 30: stabilised?

**Prepare for the future Director**
- [ ] Document installed practices: onboarding guide for the future Head/Director
- [ ] Document Baptiste's profile: strengths, development areas, autonomy level reached
- [ ] Recommendation to Jeremy on the ideal Director profile (complementary to Baptiste)
- [ ] Transition: position Fabrice as available reference point for post-mission needs

**Closing report**
- [ ] Mission closure document: before/after, stabilised projects, artefacts created, Baptiste coaching outcomes, recommendations
- [ ] Presentation to Jeremy: results, what's next, recommendation on potential extension

### Day 90 Deliverables
- [ ] PMO practices guide (artefacts + process) — standalone, usable without Fabrice
- [ ] Baptiste profile documented + recommendations for his future development
- [ ] Ideal profile for the future Head/Director of Projects
- [ ] Mission closure report presented to Jeremy

## Identified Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
